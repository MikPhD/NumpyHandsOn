# 💼 Tech Jobs Radar – Global Job Market Analysis with Pandas

Welcome to **Tech Jobs Radar**, a data-driven exploration of the global job market in the tech industry.  
This project uses real-world datasets and Pandas to extract insights on job demand, required skills, salaries, and geographic trends for roles in Data Science, AI, Software Engineering, DevOps, and beyond.

---

## 🧠 Problem Statement

In today's fast-evolving tech world, job seekers, employers, and HR departments face a common challenge:

> **Where are the best tech opportunities located, and what skills are in highest demand?**

This repository tackles this question by analyzing thousands of job listings, salary surveys, and macroeconomic indicators across different countries and years.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze the **distribution and evolution** of job opportunities in the tech sector
- Discover the **most in-demand skills** across roles and regions
- Explore **salary patterns** across countries, industries, and job titles
- Compare **remote vs on-site** roles and trends over time
- Provide a **clean and extensible data pipeline** using `pandas` and `numpy`

---

## 📊 Datasets Used

| Dataset Name | Description | Source |
|--------------|-------------|--------|
| `ai_jobs.csv` | AI, ML, and tech job listings with location, salary, and skills | [Kaggle – AI Jobs](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/PromptCloudHQ/us-technology-jobs-on-h1b-visas) |
| `remote_jobs.csv` | Remote job postings in tech from 2020–2023 | [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/) |
| `worldbank_country_stats.csv` | Country-level stats: GDP, internet use, population | [World Bank Open Data](https://data.worldbank.org/) |
